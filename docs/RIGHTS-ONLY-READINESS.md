# Linear A method parity and rights frontier

Lydian is maintained to the same evidence-control standard as Linear A while preserving the distinction between a licensed digital reference corpus and an independently collated critical edition.

## Current lawful machine layer
- complete frozen eDiAna directory capture: 118 document IDs and 471 nonempty source lines;
- source-specific readings retain eDiAna CC BY-SA 4.0;
- source-lineage register separates Gusmani, Melchert/TITUS, eDiAna and archaeological object-catalogue roles;
- disagreements and identity collisions are explicit;
- component rights matrix and residual blocker ledger;
- source-bound expert review packets;
- rights-allowlisted offline browser;
- read-only research API and loss-aware exports;
- reproducible governance/validation and parity checks.

## Counting and independence
eDiAna document IDs, catalogue headings, publication numbers and physical objects are different units. The 118 acquired document IDs are not asserted to be 118 independently verified inscriptions.

TITUS explicitly bases numbering/readings on Gusmani and sometimes selects readings from his apparatus. eDiAna describes its corpus as an adaptation of Melchert, itself mostly based on Gusmani. These interfaces therefore cannot be multiplied into independent epigraphic witnesses without record-specific evidence.

Sardis archaeological records can corroborate object identity, museum inventory, context, material and bibliography where stated. If their inscription text or translation points to Gusmani/older editions, that textual content remains in the inherited reading lineage.

## Critical evidence-growth frontier
Systematic primary-edition collation remains constrained by rights/access to Gusmani 1964 and 1980–1986 and by unestablished systematic redistribution terms for TITUS/Melchert-derived text. Sardis photographs/catalogue content carry item/content rights and are not copied merely because publicly viewable. Later publications are retained as leads until text/object concordance and reuse rights are established.

Expert epigraphic/object adjudication remains a downstream scientific gate after admissible evidence exists; it is not unfinished corpus engineering.
